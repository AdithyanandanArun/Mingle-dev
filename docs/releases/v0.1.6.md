Mingle 0.1.6 adds update notices and a Linux desktop upgrade flow.

- **App updates.** Mingle checks for newer releases and shows an update notice in the app, with progress while the update is installed and a restart notice when it is ready.
- **Linux desktop upgrades.** Packaged Linux installs can download and verify the matching native installer, then apply the upgrade and restart Mingle.
- **Provider updates.** Provider update notices are more compact and stay beside the left sidebar.
